**Waits no longer stall until the reconcile timer when a state event is
missed** — with the events hub on, `create()`, `start()` and `wait()` could
end only at the ~60 s reconcile when the socket delivered no event for the
transition, and a `create()` whose wait budget ran out first returned a
sandbox that had already started as still starting. The hub now
re-subscribes every id a wait is watching once it has heard no state frame
for half the wait's age, between 1 s and 3 s (about 1, 2, 4 and 8 s into a
wait, then every ~3 s); a wait older than a minute, or one on a socket that
is delivering events, is asked again at most every ~15 s. An id whose
previous answer is still due, or that is queued for a refused retry, is not
asked again. A missed event now delays a create that outlives the server's
hold by about 1–2 s, and any wait under a minute by at most about 4 s. At
most about 1,500 ids per second are re-subscribed per client and region,
under the server's subscribe rate. No extra HTTP requests are made.
