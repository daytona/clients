**Python: idle connections above 20 are no longer closed at once** — with
`connection_pool_maxsize` unset, each connection pool of the `v2` client still
holds up to 100 connections and now keeps up to 100 of them idle for reuse
(it kept 20), subject to httpx's keep-alive expiry, so
a burst of more than 20 concurrent calls does not pay a new connection
handshake for every extra call right after it.
